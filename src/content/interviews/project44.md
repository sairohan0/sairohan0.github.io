## The process

I was reached out by a recruiter on LinkedIn. And he sent me a test link.

## Round by round

## Online Assessment - 2025-09-03

There were two questions in the online assessment. First one was normal counting problem. Second one was a good question. It was a dp question. I had fun working on this problem. This built a good foundation of intution of dp to me.
Because there was one hour 30 min time limit, and first question was easy and I have done it in 10 min, I have ample time for the second question to work with.

I cleared this round and was called for technical rounds.

## Round 1 - 2025-09-09

This was a dsa round, The interviewer was [Vivek Jain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vivek-jain-16957a56). He asked me what kind of work I am looking for and why I want to switch. He asked me two questions, first one was normal array based counting problem. Second one was a dp question, kind of like coin combination in cses, I was not able to solve it at that time. 

I was pushed to next rounds. there were three technical rounds scheduled, DSA, LLD and Hiring Manager round.


## Round 2 - 2025-09-15

This was a dsa round, The interviewer was [Aakash Jain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aakash-jain-598482147). He was nice and interactive throught interview, I didn't do well this round. My first question was a query question. The key idea was that there will repition of queries so you have to precompute things. I didn't do well. 
And the second question was a query range question, how many pairs exist in array, such that sum of pair lies between given range. I was going to the two pointer sum approach after sorting, I would have solved it I guess. But the interviewer wanted me to go to the binary search of array for each element, like lower_bound approach, I was not comfortable with that approach. Then he told me to implement the binary search.

The round was a mess. I didn't do well at all

## Round 3 - 2025-09-16

This was a LLD round, The interviewer was [Ankur Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ankurcsa). I was asked classic question Parking Lot. I did implement functions and what was expected of system to perform. But there was no proper design , modularity, inheritence, things like that.
This was mess too.

## Round 4 - 2025-09-17

The interviewer was [Gurleen Singh](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gurleen-singh-eng)
I was asked about the project I have done in my current job and was asked to explain architecture and what would I have done differently,


I was rejected.

## What I would do differently

- I am not prepared for lld i believe, And for dsa, i still have problems with my implementation. overall it was a nice experience. Also I read before interview team culture was not nice, so I was not leaning on joining company before itself and it affected my preparation I believe. I shouldn't have done that, I should be fully prepared for the interview , regardless.
