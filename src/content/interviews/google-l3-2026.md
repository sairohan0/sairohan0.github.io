## The process

Recruiter call, then a techical phone screen, googleness round ,followed by an onsite interview with 2 rounds.

## Round by round

## Technical Phone Screen - 7th December 2025

The technical phone screen lasted about 45 minutes. The interviewer was Korean working in google search. The first question was simply maintaing the frequencies of elements. And the extension of that question was to say if the graph is a connected or not. you can do by simple dfs. I coded it good.

## Googleness Round - 10th December 2025

Asked normal googleness questions. I was able to answer them well. I was genuine in my answers. Didn't do any star method.

## Onsite Interview 1 - 24th april 2026

I was asked 3 questions , all extensions of previous one. i did first two well. I guess I struggled in third one, i wouldn't know exactly. I guessed it right. Overall I thought it went okay,

## Onsite Interview 2 - 24th april 2026

This was where I flopped so badly. I was struck for a long time. The naive solution was O(n^3) and I am able to come to O(n^2), but after few months I am able to get O(nlogn) soultion, interviewer was expectong that. This combines multiple topics, tree, backtracking, dfs, prefix sums, hash maps, binary search. I am not able to think about backtracking in the interview.

## What I would do differently

- I should have done more different types of dsa questions. Also I should have communicated with interviewer more instead of being silent and getting stuck in the last intevriew. stress also played a role. I should have not been stressful.
