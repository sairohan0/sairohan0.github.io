## The process

I was reached out by a recruiter on LinkedIn. It was University aquissition team, I was told apply on a link and after that shortly I was sent an assesment. 

## Round by round

## Online Assessment 

There were two sections in the online assesment, first section is dsa, there were two questions asked in dsa, first is normal counting problem, second one needed map of priotity queues something like that. I solved both good, able to pass all test cases.
Second section was behvaioral questions, it was long but an interesting section.
I cleared this sections and was called for technical rounds.

## Technical Round 1 

There were two female interviewers, one was senior and other was shadowing her guess. The main interviewer was very nice and seemed knowledgeable, and was very interactive throughtout the interview,

I was asked 2 questions.
First question was course prerequisie problem, if a student can take all courses or not. its a simple dfs to check if there is a cycle or not. and then i was asked about the difference between dfs and bfs, and then can't we solve this problem using dfs, and time complexity and space complexities. I guess I messed a bit up in complexities. Also my coding was good, but could have done better, i started to assign numbers to courses first, instead I should have directly used a map to store course names directly.

Second question was about a tree, it is like a level order traversal, i told the correct idea and try to implemenet it, i was going to implement the correct solution but time got over and interviewer got the idea what I was doing. In this one , i may have arrived at correct solution with optimal time complexity, but my implemenetation and approach and idea and way of explanation were all messy.

Then i was asked Amazon STAR questions. here i didn't communicate cleary or properly , i talked too fast without proper sentences in mind.

I was rejected in this round.

## What I would do differently

- This was my first interview after 16 months of gap and I was bit rusty, But still I should have practiced to arrive at clear solutions faster and to be able to write clean code. I should also look at simplicity of idea along with correctness and optimal approach,
